## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my data stored anywhere besides my device?**
No. Everything you add to Cravnoms — pantry items, budget info, favorites, custom recipes — stays on your device only. See our [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/joehainsworth/cravnoms-legal/blob/main/privacy-policy.md) for details.

**I found a bug — what should I include when I report it?**
Please include your device model, iOS version, and a quick description of what happened and what you expected instead. Screenshots help a lot too.

**Will Cravnoms be available on Android?**
Yes, an Android version is planned. Stay tuned.

**How do I back up my data or move it to a new device?**
Use the Export feature in the app's settings to save a backup file, then use Import on your new device to restore it.
