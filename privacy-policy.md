# Privacy Policy for Cravnoms

**Last updated: July 26, 2026**

Cravnoms ("the app") is developed by DarkRoast Apps. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

## Data Collection

Cravnoms does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All data you enter into the app — including pantry contents, budget information, favorite recipes, and any custom ingredients or recipes you add — is stored **locally on your device only**, using your device's local storage.

We do not have access to this data. It is never sent to us or to any third party.

## Third-Party Services

Cravnoms displays recipe images loaded from a third-party content delivery network (food.com's image CDN). Loading these images may share standard technical information (such as your IP address) with that provider, consistent with normal web image loading. We do not control this data flow and it is not used by us for any purpose.

## Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the app or clearing its data through your device settings. The app also provides an in-app export/import feature so you can back up or transfer your own data at your discretion.

## Children's Privacy

Cravnoms does not knowingly collect data from children, as it does not collect data from anyone — all information stays on-device.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes (for example, if future features involve server-side data, such as an optional AI-powered scanning feature), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
